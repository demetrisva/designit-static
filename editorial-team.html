EDITORIAL_STRUCTURE

DesignIt.pro is run as a small editorial desk rather than a high-volume content operation. The site exists to publish careful technical analysis, not a stream of lightly rewritten posts.

EDITORIAL ROLES

  • Managing editor: sets scope, decides whether the topic is worth publishing, and signs off on corrections.
  • Technical reviewer: challenges assumptions, checks rollout advice, and pushes back on vague recommendations.
  • Research editor: validates source quality and makes sure the links point to the underlying documentation rather than commentary about it.

REVIEW_STANDARD

  • Every report gets at least one technical review pass before publication.
  • Security and incident-response pieces require an additional verification pass because the failure cost is higher.
  • Material updates are reflected in both the article body and the metadata.

CONTRIBUTOR_EXPECTATIONS

Useful submissions include attributable evidence, direct source links, reproducible context, and a clear description of the operational constraint. Promotional pitches, vague trend pieces, and unverified claims are rejected.

WHY_THIS_PAGE_EXISTS

Readers should be able to understand who is responsible for the site and how quality control works. This page exists to make that structure explicit rather than implied.

CORRECTIONS_AND_CONTACT

If you identify a factual issue, submit a correction through contact.html with the page URL, quote, and source evidence. Corrections are prioritized over general feedback.
