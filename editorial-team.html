PUBLICATION MODEL

DesignIt.pro is an independent technical publication focused on practical engineering reports. We publish explainers and incident guidance for developers, operators, and technical leads.

EDITORIAL ROLES

  • Managing Editor: Approves story scope, quality threshold, and correction notices.
  • Technical Reviewer: Verifies implementation guidance and failure-mode assumptions.
  • Research Editor: Validates sources and ensures links point to primary documentation.

REVIEW REQUIREMENTS

  • Each report requires at least one technical review pass before publication.
  • Critical security or incident content requires an additional verification check.
  • Material updates are date-stamped in the article body and metadata.

CONTRIBUTOR STANDARDS

We only accept contributions that include attributable evidence, reproducible context, and clear operational constraints. Promotional submissions without technical value are rejected.

CORRECTIONS AND CONTACT

If you identify a factual issue, submit a correction through contact.html with the page URL, quote, and source evidence. Correction requests are prioritized.
