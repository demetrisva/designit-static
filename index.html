HOW_THIS_SITE_WORKS

REPORTING

We start from primary documentation, incident writeups, standards, release notes, and repeated implementation problems seen across working teams.

ANALYSIS

Each report is written as an editorial analysis, not an automated summary. The goal is to explain what changed, why it matters, and what teams should actually do.

REVIEW

We review claims against the source material and keep update dates visible when a page changes materially.

SCOPE

We publish fewer pages than a news feed on purpose. The site focuses on topics where implementation details are more valuable than volume.
