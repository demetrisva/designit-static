1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

We collect information you choose to send to us, such as your name, email address, and the contents of your message when you use the contact form.

We also receive limited technical data that is routinely generated when the site is requested, such as IP address, browser and device information, request path, referrer data, and approximate access time through our hosting, CDN, analytics, advertising, and form-processing providers.

2. HOW WE USE INFORMATION

  • To respond to editorial, privacy, correction, and support requests.
  • To maintain site security, detect abuse, and preserve service availability.
  • To run advertising and consent flows where those are enabled.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations and enforce our terms.

3. LEGAL BASES

Depending on the context, we process personal data because it is necessary to respond to your request, because it is necessary for our legitimate interests in operating and securing the site, because you have given consent where consent is required, or because the law requires us to do so.

4. SERVICE PROVIDERS AND TRANSFERS

We use third-party services to operate the site, including hosting and CDN infrastructure, Formspree for contact submissions, Google AdSense and related consent tools for advertising, and externally hosted font or icon assets. These providers may process data outside your country, subject to their own infrastructure choices and contractual arrangements.

5. ADVERTISING, COOKIES, AND CONSENT

We use Google AdSense to display advertising. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, personalize, and measure ads, subject to the consent and legal framework that applies in the visitor's location. See our Cookies Policy for more detail.

6. DATA RETENTION

We retain personal data only for as long as it is reasonably needed for the purposes described above, including correspondence handling, security review, record-keeping, and legal compliance. Different providers may keep server or advertising logs for their own retention periods.

7. YOUR RIGHTS

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or objection in relation to your personal data. Users in the EEA and UK may also withdraw consent where consent is the legal basis, and some U.S. state privacy laws provide related rights. You can submit requests through contact.html.

8. CONTACT

If you have privacy questions, use our contact page and include "Privacy Request" in your message subject so we can route it correctly.
