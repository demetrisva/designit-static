WHAT_THIS_SITE_IS

DesignIt.pro is a small independent editorial site about software implementation work. We publish technical analysis for engineers, operators, and technical leads who need more than a short news summary.

The site focuses on topics where detail matters: frontend architecture, incident response, performance, reliability, data governance, and developer workflow.

HOW_WE_PUBLISH

Articles are written by the DesignIt.pro editorial desk and reviewed before publication for technical clarity, source quality, and practical usefulness. We do not try to mimic a high-volume news site. We publish fewer pieces and spend more time on whether the recommendation is actually usable.

Each published report carries a visible date, source references, and an update path when the guidance changes materially.

WHY_THE_CONTENT_IS_ORIGINAL

We write editorial analysis, not scraped summaries. The goal is to explain what changed, why it matters operationally, where teams usually make mistakes, and which source material supports that reading.

That means the writing intentionally reflects judgment. We care about implementation detail, rollout risk, and the practical consequences of a technical decision, not just repeating documentation in a shorter format.

EDITORIAL_RULES

  • We prioritize primary sources such as standards, advisories, release notes, incident reports, and official documentation.
  • If a claim cannot be verified, we either label it as uncertain or remove it.
  • If a page changes materially, we update the article date and revise the recommendation in public.

CORRECTIONS POLICY

If a page contains an error, we correct it and keep the change visible through the update date and revised copy. Corrections can be submitted through the contact page.

AI AND AUTOMATION DISCLOSURE

Automation may be used for research support, formatting, and drafting assistance. Final editorial judgment, fact checking, and publication approval are handled by a human reviewer.

MONETIZATION DISCLOSURE

DesignIt.pro uses advertising, including Google AdSense, to support hosting and editorial work. Advertising does not determine coverage, conclusions, or technical recommendations.

CONTACT

Editorial questions, corrections, and source submissions can be sent through contact.html. Correction requests are prioritized.

Include the page URL, the specific statement in question, and a source we can verify. That makes review materially faster.
