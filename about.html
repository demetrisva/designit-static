MISSION

DesignIt.pro publishes practical technology reporting for engineers, operators, and technical leads. Our goal is to make complex operational issues understandable and useful, not just fast to read.

We focus on topics where implementation details matter: frontend architecture, performance, infrastructure reliability, security incidents, and data governance.

WHO WRITES AND REVIEWS

Stories are drafted by the DesignIt.pro editorial team and reviewed before publication for technical clarity, source quality, and actionable value. We avoid anonymous rumor-based posts and prioritize first-hand evidence or primary documentation.

HOW WE REPORT

  • We prioritize primary sources such as official documentation, security advisories, release notes, incident reports, and direct maintainer statements.
  • If a claim cannot be verified, we either label it clearly as unconfirmed or remove it.
  • When meaningful new information appears, we update the article and adjust guidance.

CORRECTIONS POLICY

If we publish inaccurate technical details, we correct the page as quickly as possible and keep the update transparent in the article metadata. You can report potential errors through our contact page.

AI AND AUTOMATION DISCLOSURE

We may use automation tools to assist formatting, drafting support material, or summarization. Final editorial decisions, fact checks, and publication approvals are handled by a human reviewer.

MONETIZATION DISCLOSURE

DesignIt.pro uses advertising, including Google AdSense, to support site operations. Advertising does not determine editorial conclusions, coverage decisions, or technical recommendations.

CONTACT

Editorial questions, corrections, and policy concerns can be sent via contact.html. We review submissions continuously and prioritize correction requests.
