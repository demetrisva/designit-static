CRA_SHIPPING_CALENDAR

2026-03-28 7 MIN READ PRODUCT SECURITY

The Cyber Resilience Act is now something product teams can schedule against, not just a future legal acronym. The important dates are no longer vague: vulnerability reporting obligations arrive earlier, and the broader regime follows after that.

KEY_DATES

Under the CRA, manufacturers' reporting obligations for actively exploited vulnerabilities and severe incidents apply from 11 September 2026, while most of the wider requirements apply from 11 December 2027.

WHY_THE_CALENDAR_MATTERS_NOW

Teams tend to hear 2027 and assume there is plenty of time. In practice, the hard work lands much earlier: asset inventories, vulnerability intake, SBOM discipline, release evidence, and a documented path from product issue to reportable event.

By the time the deadline arrives, the organization will need more than a policy PDF. It will need working process, named owners, and a product architecture that can answer basic questions about components, dependencies, and remediation state.

"Regulation lands as workflow long before it lands as paperwork."

WHAT_MANUFACTURERS_AND_TEAMS_SHOULD_BE_BUILDING

The CRA asks for product security that is visible across the lifecycle, not hidden inside specialist teams. For software organizations, that means being able to trace what shipped, what was vulnerable, who owned the fix, and whether customers or authorities need notice.

  • Maintain a live inventory of products, supported versions, dependencies, and externally reachable components.
  • Define how vulnerability intake, triage, remediation, and public communication work in one workflow.
  • Keep release evidence that shows security fixes were assessed, shipped, and tied to the right product version.

The organizations that will struggle are not necessarily the least technical ones. They are the ones where product, security, legal, and support are all holding different pieces of the same incident without a shared operating map.

WHAT_TO_DO_BEFORE_2026

The 2026 reporting milestone is the better forcing function than the later general application date. If your internal escalation path cannot already identify a serious product-security issue, classify it, and decide whether reporting is triggered, the calendar is shorter than it looks.

  • Build product and dependency inventories that product, engineering, and security all trust.
  • Write incident criteria and reporting paths that do not depend on a single person remembering the law from memory.
  • Rehearse who gathers evidence, who approves notice, and who owns customer communications.

PRIMARY_REFERENCES

  • Cyber Resilience Act, Regulation (EU) 2024/2847
  • Consolidated CRA text on EUR-Lex
