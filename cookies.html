1. WHAT COOKIES AND SIMILAR TECHNOLOGIES ARE

Cookies and similar technologies are small identifiers stored or read by your browser to support site delivery, remember preferences, measure usage, and serve advertising.

2. HOW WE USE THEM

  • Essential technologies support core site delivery, security, and contact-form handling.
  • Advertising technologies are used by Google AdSense and its partners to serve and measure ads.
  • Third-party providers such as hosting/CDN, Formspree, Google Fonts, and Font Awesome CDN may also receive technical request data when their services load.

3. GOOGLE ADVERTISING

Google may use cookies or similar technologies to personalize ads based on your visits to this and other sites and to measure ad performance. In the EEA, UK, and Switzerland, consent may be required before non-essential advertising technologies are used.

4. MANAGING STORAGE

You can clear site storage and any previously saved data through your browser settings. Where available, you can also use the site's Privacy choices link to reopen Google's consent message. Blocking third-party assets may change how typography or icons load, but the site should remain usable.

5. CONTACT

If you have questions about cookie usage on DesignIt.pro, contact us through contact.html.
